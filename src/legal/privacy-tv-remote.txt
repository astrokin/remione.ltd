Privacy Policy

Smart TV Remote Control Application  |  Effective date: 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Remione LTD processes information when you use the Smart TV remote control application. The App connects your iPhone or iPad to compatible televisions and media devices over the same Wi-Fi or local network.

Remione LTD (“Remione”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is the data controller for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy, except where a third-party service acts as an independent controller. This Policy applies to the mobile application, our website at remione.ltd, and related support services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. About the App

The App allows users to discover, connect to, pair with, and control compatible Smart TVs, streaming devices, and media devices through a local Wi-Fi or other local network.

scan the local network for compatible devices;

identify supported devices and services;

establish direct local connections;

send remote-control commands using IP, HTTP, HTTPS, WebSocket, TCP, UDP, Bonjour, or manufacturer-specific protocols;

remember selected devices, pairing information, and preferences on the user’s device.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, remote-control commands are transmitted directly between the Apple device and the television or media device through the local network and are not routed through Remione’s servers.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Local network and television information

To provide core functionality, the App may detect or process:

local IP addresses and network service information;

television or media-device names, manufacturer, model, and supported capabilities;

device identifiers made available by the television or operating system;

pairing, authentication, and connection status;

previously selected devices and connection preferences;

remote-control commands and technical responses.

This information is generally processed locally on the user’s device. We do not use local-network access to monitor unrelated devices or activities on the network. We do not intentionally collect Wi-Fi passwords.

2.2 Information stored locally

selected television or media device;

device name, local IP address, and supported protocol;

pairing tokens or authentication information required by the television;

connection, interface, and control preferences;

information used to restore purchased functionality.

Locally stored information normally remains until the user disconnects the device, resets the App, deletes the App, or the operating system removes the information. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.

2.3 Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all App Store payments. Remione does not receive complete payment-card details. We and our subscription-management provider may receive or process:

product and subscription identifiers;

transaction identifiers and App Store receipts;

purchase, trial, renewal, expiration, cancellation, and refund status;

subscription entitlement and purchase-restoration information;

App Store storefront, currency, and limited platform information;

an anonymous or pseudonymous App User ID.

2.4 Analytics, device, and usage information

Where analytics is enabled and, where legally required, after consent, we may process:

randomly generated installation identifiers, analytics-provider identifiers, and Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) where available and used;

App launches, sessions, screens, and feature interactions;

connection attempts, supported television type, connection results, and technical error codes;

paywall, trial, purchase, subscription, renewal, and entitlement events;

App version, device model, operating-system version, language, country or region, and time zone;

attribution and advertising-campaign information where applicable;

crash, performance, and diagnostic information where diagnostic services are enabled.

Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous, not necessarily anonymous, because they may allow events from the same installation or device to be associated over time.

We do not intentionally include the following information in analytics events:

Wi-Fi or television-account passwords;

television pairing codes or authentication tokens;

the full content of text entered through the App’s keyboard;

media content displayed on the television;

communications sent through third-party television applications;

precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or address-book information.

2.5 Support communications

name and email address;

the content of the request and our response;

screenshots, logs, or attachments voluntarily provided;

App version, device model, television model, and network information supplied for troubleshooting.

Users should not send passwords, payment-card details, television account credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2.6 Website information

When a user visits remione.ltd, our hosting and security providers may process IP address, browser and operating-system information, visited pages, access time, referring page, cookie identifiers, and server or security logs. Non-essential cookies are used only where permitted by law and, where required, after consent.

3. Local Network Permission

The App requires Apple’s Local Network permission to discover and communicate with compatible devices. If permission is denied, core remote-control features may not work. Permission can be changed in iOS or iPadOS settings. Granting permission does not give Remione ownership or general control of the user’s network.

The providers used may depend on the App version, enabled features, device settings, and consent choices.

6.1 Apple

Apple operates the App Store and processes downloads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, refunds, purchase restoration, and associated account and transaction information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

6.2 RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage and validate in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat may process an anonymous or pseudonymous App User ID, product and transaction identifiers, receipts, purchase and subscription history, entitlement status, trial and promotional-offer status, renewal and expiration information, and limited platform and App information. 6.3 Amplitude

Subject to consent where required by law, we use Amplitude for product analytics. Amplitude may process pseudonymous installation or device identifiers, IDFV where available and enabled, App events and event properties, sessions, App and device information, region, feature interactions, connection outcomes, and paywall or subscription-related interactions.

6.4 Google Firebase

We use selected Google Firebase services for App operation, security, analytics, configuration, or diagnostics. Depending on the modules enabled in the applicable App version, Google may process installation identifiers, App and device information, IP address and general location, analytics events, sessions, security and App-integrity signals, diagnostic information, crash reports, and technical logs.

Firebase App Check may process device and App-integrity information to verify authentic installations and protect systems from abuse. If Google Analytics for Firebase is enabled, it may process pseudonymous identifiers, usage events, device information, purchase events, and attribution information, subject to consent where required.

6.5 Google on-device conversion measurement

Where enabled, Google’s on-device conversion measurement may be used to measure whether advertising campaigns result in installations, subscriptions, or other conversion events. It may process limited advertising, installation, device, and conversion information. We request App Tracking Transparency permission where Apple’s rules require it. Denying tracking permission does not prevent use of the App’s core remote-control functionality.

6.6 Technical libraries

The App also contains libraries used for networking, encryption, logging, local storage, interface layout, dependency management, compression, and similar technical functions. Inclusion of a library does not necessarily mean that its developer receives personal data. A provider is separately disclosed where it receives or independently processes personal data from the App.

9. Data Retention

Local television and pairing information: until the device is disconnected, the App is reset, or the App is deleted, unless the television or operating system retains related information independently.

Analytics and product-usage data: according to the retention period configured in the relevant provider, generally no longer than 24 months unless a shorter period is selected or longer retention is legally justified.

Diagnostic and security logs under Remione’s control: normally no longer than 12 months, unless needed to investigate an active incident.

Support communications: generally up to 3 years after the request is closed, unless longer retention is necessary for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation.

Purchase, tax, and accounting records: for the period required by applicable law, generally at least 6 years where Cyprus record-retention rules apply.

Aggregated or irreversibly anonymised information: may be retained longer because it no longer identifies an individual.

Third-party providers may apply their own retention periods. We configure and manage provider retention settings where available and delete or de-identify information when it is no longer required.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, organisational, and technical measures designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission, local-network protocol, or storage system is completely secure.

Some televisions rely on unencrypted local HTTP or manufacturer-specific protocols. Users are responsible for securing their Wi-Fi network, router, television, and connected devices and should avoid controlling devices through unknown or public networks.
